EMBERVALE ATLAS

Surface world

Trails connect the expanded regions, lakes, and cave entrances. Town markers show Hearthside, Fernhaven, Lotus Rest, and Aurora Camp. Buildings offer shops, blacksmiths, house loot, and some casinos. Approach a doorway or cave mouth and press E; underground descents lead to deeper caves.

THE WORKBENCH

Crafting & equipment

Click bushes and pebbles to gather. Craft a Crude Stone Axe with 2 sticks + 2 stones, then click trees for wood. Craft a campfire to cook meat or fish. Craft a fishing rod and click lake water to cast from shore. Select items with 1–6. Find Marlow on the eastern beach for ten fishing quests. J opens the journal; V dives or surfaces. Build summit shacks and useful machines. R rotates walls and gates.

Every resource, tool, armor piece, and structure is listed here. Items you do not currently carry stay in this guide.
