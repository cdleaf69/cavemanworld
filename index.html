LEAF · STONE · YOUR FIRST ADVENTURE

Quick start

  1. 1

    Gather the basics

    WASD moves, Shift runs, Space jumps. Click nearby bushes and pebbles for sticks, leaves and stone.

  2. 2

    Craft & equip

    C shows recipes and costs. Craft an axe: 2 sticks + 2 stone. In I, equip tools or add them to slots 1–6. Axe → wood; pickaxe → ore.

  3. 3

    Earn your upgrades

    Harvesting, combat and quests give XP. Watch your level bar. Craft a Bark Workbench at level 3, then higher tables to unlock stronger recipes.

  4. 4

    Eat & survive

    Food heals. E at a campfire: add wood + raw meat/fish, collect cooked food. Eat from I or your hotbar. Equip armor for protection and perks.

  5. 5

    Build your base

    Craft floors, walls, roofs and machines. Add a kit to slots 1–6; select it and click clear ground nearby. R rotates walls/gates. E uses machines.

  6. 6

    Explore & find quests

    E: NPCs, buildings and caves. J: quests. M: map. Deeper caves have better ore and tougher mobs. Meet the beach fisherman! Rod: click water, then click repeatedly on a bite. V dives/surfaces; watch your breath.
