Surface world

Pick up pebbles and sticks around the hearth. Bushes give sticks and leaves by hand. Trees need an axe; boulders need a pickaxe. Click each node to gather. Put a crafted Fishing Rod on your hotbar, then click lake water from shore. When the bobber bites, click it or press E to catch a fish. A campfire needs wood fuel to cook raw meat or fish. Click it with a pickaxe three times to recover it. Resources regrow. Zone boundaries can only be edited in developer mode (?dev=1). Multiplayer is not connected.
